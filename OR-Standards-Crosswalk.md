# **Narrative Crosswalk for Aligning the CIS Safeguards to the Oregon Statewide Standards and Plan**

**Note:** throughout this document, all page references to "Statewide Standards" refer to the [2023 Statewide Information Technology Control Standards](https://www.oregon.gov/eis/cyber-security-services/Documents/eis-css-statewide-information-technology%28IT%29-control-standards.pdf); page references to the "Statewide Plan" refer to the [2023 Statewide Information Security Program Plan](https://www.oregon.gov/eis/cyber-security-services/Documents/eis-css-statewide-information-security-program-plan.pdf). Not all Safeguards listed below are currently assessed, and not all assessed Safeguards have a direct correlation with the Statewide Standards.

# CIS Control 1

## CIS Safeguard 1.01

The CIS Safeguard 1.01 requirement “maintain an accurate, detailed, and up-to-date inventory of all enterprise assets” is broader than the Statewide Standards Control CM-8(1) requirement “Update the inventory of system components as part of component installations, removals, and system updates” (20). The CIS Safeguard 1.01 requirement “maintain an accurate, detailed, and up-to-date inventory of all enterprise assets” is narrower than the Statewide Standards Control CM-8 requirement “develop and document an inventory of system components that accurately reflects the current system” (20), and the Statewide Plan Section 19.1.4 PM-5 requirement "develop and maintain an inventory of its information systems" (14).

## CIS Safeguard 1.02

The CIS Safeguard 1.02 requirement “a process existing to address unauthorized assets on a weekly basis” is narrower than the Statewide Standards Control CM-8(3) requirement “detect the presence of unauthorized hardware, software, and firmware components...and remove or quarantine unauthorized components” (20).

## CIS Safeguard 1.03

The CIS Safeguard 1.03 requirement “utilize an active discovery tool to identify assets connected to the enterprise's network” is narrower than the Statewide Standards Control SI-4 requirement “monitor events to detect...unauthorized local, network, and remote connections” (55).

## CIS Safeguard 1.04

The CIS Safeguard 1.04 requirement “use DHCP logging...to update the enterprise's asset inventory” is narrower than the Statewide Standards Control CM-8(3) requirement “detect the presence of unauthorized hardware, software, and firmware components...and remove or quarantine unauthorized components” (20).

## CIS Safeguard 1.05

The CIS Safeguard 1.05 requirement “use a passive discovery tool to identify assets connected to the enterprise's network” is narrower than the Statewide Standards Control CM-8(3) requirement “detect the presence of unauthorized hardware, software, and firmware components...and remove or quarantine unauthorized components” (20), and the Statewide Standards Control SI-4 requirement “monitor events to detect...unauthorized local, network, and remote connections” (55).
